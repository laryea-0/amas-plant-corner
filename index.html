A little help from Ama

Plants have preferences.

The small things that make a big difference. Tap a tip to get the simple version, then ask Ama if you are still unsure.

01 Plants hate soggy shoes. +

Let the top inch of soil dry before watering again. Make sure excess water can escape through the pot so roots can breathe.

02 Bright does not mean blazing. +

Most houseplants love bright, indirect light. A little morning sun is kind; hot afternoon rays through glass can scorch leaves.

03 Leaves like a clean start. +

Dust can block light. Wipe broad leaves gently with a damp cloth every few weeks and check underneath for tiny pests.

04 Roots need room to settle. +

A new plant does not need a bigger pot straight away. Give it time to adjust, then repot when roots circle the bottom.

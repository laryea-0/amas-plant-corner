# Additional improvement

## Newsletter sign-up in the footer

Beyond the brief's core page sections, the redesign adds a newsletter sign-up
to the footer. The form asks only for an email address and is positioned after
the visitor has seen Ama's products, advice, and personality, so it is a
natural next step rather than an interruption.

This adds business value by giving Ama a way to stay connected with people who
are interested but are not ready to visit that day. She could use the list to
announce new plant arrivals, seasonal care reminders, workshops, or short
neighbourhood updates. It also adds customer value by making useful plant
guidance available after the first visit.

The current implementation includes the accessible email field, native email
validation, and a clear submit control. It does not pretend that delivery is
already implemented: the form needs to be connected to Ama's chosen email
service before it should collect real subscribers.
